Climate

Equatorial — humid and warm year-round

Average temperatures stay between 25–28 °C with high rainfall (2,000–3,000 mm annually). The forest itself recycles ~75% of its own rainfall.

Biodiversity

Earth's most species-rich biome

Home to roughly 10% of all known species — 40,000 plant species, 2.5 million insect species, 2,000 bird and mammal species.

Importance

"Lungs of the planet"

Acts as one of the largest carbon sinks on Earth — absorbs roughly 2 billion tonnes of CO₂ per year while producing 6% of the world's oxygen.

Countries

Spans 9 nations

Brazil holds ~60% of the rainforest. Peru, Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Bolivia, Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana hold the rest.

Fun Facts

It used to flow the other way

~10 million years ago the Amazon River flowed westward into the Pacific — until the Andes rose and reversed its direction toward the Atlantic.

Threats

Deforestation pressure

~17% of the original forest has been lost. Scientists warn that a 20–25% loss could push the ecosystem past a tipping point into savanna.
