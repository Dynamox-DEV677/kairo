Delete your account & data

You can delete your Kyno account and all associated data at any time. Here's how.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Kyno and go to Settings.
  2. Under Privacy & Data, tap “Clear all data & reset” — this erases everything stored on your device (profile, progress, notes, XP).
  3. Under Backup & Sync, tap “Delete cloud copy” to remove the backup stored in your Kyno account.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete everything

To fully delete your account (including your sign-in and any cloud data), email us from your registered address:

kairoindustries.cor@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my account. We'll permanently delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (email / Google login)
  • Your profile — name, class, board, goals
  • Your study data — questions, progress, XP, notes, flashcards, mistakes
  • Your cloud backup

Nothing personal is kept after deletion. We may retain only anonymous, aggregated usage counts that can't identify you.
