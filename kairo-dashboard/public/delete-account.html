Delete your Kyno account

Kyno: AI Academic Twin, by Kairo Industries · Last updated: 23 October 2026

You can delete your Kyno account and the data we hold about you at any time. There are two ways.

1. In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Kyno and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away. There is no undo.

2. By email (if you can't open the app)

Email kairoindustries.cor@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Kyno account”, from the email address you signed up with. We will delete the account and its data and reply to confirm, within 30 days at the latest.

A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's account the same way.

Delete some of your data, keep your account

  • Your cloud backup — Profile → Backup and devices → Delete my cloud copy. This removes the copy of your study data stored in your Kyno account. Your account stays, and your work stays on this phone.
  • Data on this phone — Profile → This device → Clear everything on this device. This erases your study history, flashcards and settings from the phone. Your account is not affected.
  • Anything else — email us at the address below and say what you want removed. We will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted when you delete your account

  • Your sign-in (email and password, or the Google sign-in link).
  • Your profile: name, username, class, board and school.
  • Your study data on our servers: progress and mastery, study sessions, notes, exam plans, AI memory, league and battle records, reports and feedback you sent.
  • Your cloud backup (the copy that syncs your study data between devices).

What stays, and where

  • Data on your own phone — study data saved on the device, and textbooks you added to the Reader, stay there until you uninstall Kyno or clear its storage.
  • Routine backups of our database may hold a copy for up to 30 days before they expire.
