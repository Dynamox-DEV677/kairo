Privacy Policy

Kyno, by Kairo Industries · Last updated: 14 July 2026

Kyno is an AI-powered learning app for students. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to help you learn.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name and email, when you sign up or sign in (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Profile you provide — nickname, class/grade, board, school (optional), how you like to study, subjects, goals and interests you enter during setup.
  • Learning activity — questions you ask, quizzes, notes, flashcards, and progress. This powers your personal "academic twin" so Kyno can adapt to you.
  • On-device data — most of your study data is stored locally on your device and synced so it follows you across devices.

How we use it

  • To provide and personalise your learning experience (explanations, revision suggestions, your twin and stats).
  • To operate features like the AI Solver, notebook, flashcards and leaderboard.
  • To keep the app secure and improve it.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase — secure account sign-in and database.
  • Groq & OpenRouter — AI providers that generate answers. Your questions are sent to these services to produce responses.
  • Google — optional Google Sign-In.
  • Email — to send account and account-related messages.

These providers process data only to deliver their part of the service.

Children & students

Kyno is built for students, some of whom may be young. We collect only what's needed for learning and never require unnecessary personal details. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, please use Kyno with a parent or guardian's permission. Parents can contact us to review or delete a child's data.

Your choices & data deletion

  • You can edit your profile any time in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us (below); we will delete it promptly.
  • Clearing the app's storage removes locally-held study data on that device.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard it.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll change the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
